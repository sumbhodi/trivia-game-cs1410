# Trivia Game · the YouTube ad (draft script, for both of us to change)

About 75 seconds. The cast is our own avatars: the snow leopard (the player), the fox (the band's bass drummer), the robot (the salesman),
and the owl (the grad who says no). Pictures: see "Trivia Game ad - storyboard.png". Nothing is filmed yet; this is the draft.

---

**1. Touchdown (8 s).**

A night game under the lights. The snow leopard breaks a tackle and dives over the goal line.

---

**2. The roar (4 s).**

It rips its helmet off and roars. Confetti.

---

**3. The band takes the field (6 s).**

The fox drummer leads the marching band on. The drum line kicks in.

---

**4. The concession stand (4 s).**

The robot behind the counter, a pyramid of floppy disks, a cardboard sign: "TRIVIA GAME · $9.99 · MIRACLES".

---

**5. The pitch (25 s).**

The robot hawks to the crowd, each claim floating up on screen in a snake-oil font.

> ROBOT: Ten seconds of Trivia Game cures THE VAPORS! Dropsy! Lumbago! Brain fever!

> ROBOT: Eliminates biliousness, the grippe, and the dreaded Floppy Disk Elbow!

The owl walks up, picks the manual up off the counter, and starts reading it.

> ROBOT: Grows hair! Straightens teeth! Raises your IQ by forty points, per question!

> ROBOT: Four out of five doctors agree! The fifth was not asked!

The robot glances at the owl, nervous. A bead of sweat.

> ROBOT: It realigns your chakras, your credit score AND your car's tires! It changes your perception of REAL—

> OWL *(not looking up from the manual; disaffected, flat)*: It doesn't do any of that.

The robot tries again. Every claim gets a flat "No." and a rimshot from the fox.

> ROBOT: Ends world hunger!

> OWL: No.

> ROBOT: Fixes climate change!

> OWL: No.

> ROBOT: Makes you younger! Thinner!

> OWL: No.

> ROBOT: …Thicker in all the right places?

> OWL: No.

> ROBOT: Smarter! A better driver! Better looking! Better at CHESS!

> OWL: No. No. No. Definitely no.

---

**6. "Then what IS it?" (8 s).**

> OWL *(reading the box)*: It's a portfolio piece. It shows two students can work as a team and use Java properly.

> ROBOT: So what does it DO?

> OWL: It mildly distracts you. For up to ninety seconds.

---

**7. The honest pitch (20 s).**

The robot climbs onto a crate. A glowing brain lights up beside him, one region at a time. He's still a salesman, but now every word is true
(it's all in the owl's manual: "Trivia Game - fact sheet.md").

> ROBOT *(straightening up)*: Okay. Okay. What it REALLY does.

> ROBOT: Every question snaps your attention right here, right now. That's your orienting response!

> ROBOT: One question, four answers, all in your head at once. Working memory, right at its limit!

> ROBOT: Your hippocampus goes digging through everything you've ever learned. That tip-of-the-tongue feeling? That's the search!

> ROBOT: Your hand finds the button without looking. Proprioception! Fitts's law! We made the buttons big ON PURPOSE!

> ROBOT: Get one wrong, your brain flags it. Get one right, a little hit of dopamine!

The brain glows brighter. The crowd goes quiet.

> ROBOT *(softer)*: And for ninety seconds, the part of your brain that worries takes a break. You're not thinking about rent. You're thinking
> about the Pacific Ocean.

> ROBOT: It doesn't change reality. It changes where your attention is. Which, if you think about it… is the same thing.

The owl lowers the manual. A beat.

> OWL: …Technically correct.

---

**8. The funnel (6 s).**

> OWL *(sighs)*: There really isn't a monetization funnel here.

> ROBOT: Then I'll make one. Can we make it shaped like a Klein bottle?

Cut, right on that line.

---

**9. One real round (about 40 s).**

No voice, no music: just the sounds of play. Someone types the username "no", picks an avatar, answers three questions (one wrong, grey X),
and lands on the high scores.

Then a two-second card: Trivia Game · Jaims & Cooper · CS 1410 · made with AI.
