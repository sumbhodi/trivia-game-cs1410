# Trivia Game ad · the plan (draft, 20 Nov)

## How the clips chain
Each clip is made from a START picture and an END picture (Gemini Omni in Leonardo takes both). Each clip's END is the next clip's START, so
the cut is seamless. Pictures: "Trivia Game ad - keyframes (the football open).png" and the "ad keyframes" folder.

| clip | start → end | what happens | sound | about |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A → B | the snow leopard, helmet on, runs down the sideline and dives into the end zone | crowd, cleats, the thud | 6 s |
| 2 | B → C | it gets up, rips its helmet off and roars; confetti, fireworks, the band jumping on the sideline | the roar, the crowd erupts, fireworks | 6 s |
| 3 | C → D | it jogs off, waving; the band marches onto the field | the crowd, the band's first notes | 6 s |
| 4 | D → E | the drumline kicks in, then a hard cut to halftime at the concession stand | the drumline | 4 s |
| 5+ | the concession stand (the script's scenes 4–8) | the robot, the owl, the honest pitch, the Klein bottle | voices | **waits on Cooper's notes** |
| last | the real round as "no" | the rig plays the game | keys, clicks, the game's sounds | 40 s |

## Voices and sound
- The football open: each clip makes its own sound (crowd, roar, drums). No dialogue.
- The concession scenes have dialogue. Three ways, our pick: we record the robot and owl lines ourselves (live), AI voices, or a mix.
  If the voices are recorded after the video, a lip-sync pass matches the mouths to the new sound (there are lip-sync models on
  Replicate, and Leonardo has the key; which one gets checked when we get there).
- The science words in the honest pitch are put on screen after, so they're spelled right.

## Cost and order
- Now (ready): clips 1–4, the football open: 4 clips, plus a retake or two.
- After Cooper's notes: the concession scenes, about 5–6 clips.
- The real round: no credits (the rig records the real game).
- Every batch gets a clip count before it runs.
